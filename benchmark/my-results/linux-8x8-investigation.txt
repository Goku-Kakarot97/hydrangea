spinning before epoll_wait (experiment, removed): loops=4 conns=8 62 372 -> 93 093 with 100 us of spin; loops=8 conns=16 104 598 -> 112 595 (8 spinning loops on a 4-CPU quota: confounded)

## conclusion
(1) the pool's cross-loop hand-off: fixed in MyPool and PgPool;  (2) the VM's idle wake-up latency: the same for every client, documented;
(3) a loop driving exactly two connections of a thread-per-connection server on this VM runs at about 60% of a loop driving one: real, reproduced for both databases, absent with an echo
server and absent over a slower link; the kernel side was not investigated. Benchmarks now default to one loop for each connection.
